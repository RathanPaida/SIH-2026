TENDER NOTICE

Subject: Supply of Packaged Food Items and Drinking Water for Government Hospitals, Delhi

Tender Reference No: DGHS/DEL/2025/FOOD-0212

1. SCOPE OF SUPPLY
The Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS), Government of NCT of Delhi invites tenders for the supply of packaged food items and drinking water to 15 government hospitals across Delhi for a period of 12 months.

2. ITEM SPECIFICATIONS AND QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

2.1 PACKAGED DRINKING WATER
- 1 litre bottles: 50,000 nos./month
- 20 litre jars (for dispensers): 5,000 nos./month
- Water quality shall strictly conform to IS standards for packaged drinking water specifications
- All packaged water must meet IS requirements for drinking water quality parameters
- BIS certification (ISI mark) mandatory for all packaged water suppliers

2.2 CANNED/PACKAGED FOOD ITEMS
- Canned fruits and vegetables: Must conform to IS general requirements for canned foods
- All canned food shall have minimum 12 months remaining shelf life at the time of delivery
- Packaging and labelling as per FSSAI regulations and applicable IS standards

2.3 EDIBLE OILS
- Refined sunflower oil and mustard oil in 5 litre packs
- Packaging and labelling shall conform to IS standards for edible oil packaging
- FSSAI license number must be printed on all packs

2.4 GENERAL FOOD SAFETY REQUIREMENTS
- All food handling, storage, and transportation shall comply with IS code of practice for food hygiene
- HACCP certification is mandatory for all food processing units
- Supplier must have valid FSSAI license for all food categories being supplied
- Cold chain maintenance required for perishable items
- Temperature monitoring logs must be maintained and submitted monthly

3. QUALITY ASSURANCE AND TESTING
- Random samples will be drawn and tested at NABL accredited laboratories
- Water samples shall be tested for all parameters specified in IS standards for drinking water
- Any batch failing quality tests will be rejected and supplier will bear replacement costs
- Quarterly quality audits of supplier's processing facility

4. DELIVERY REQUIREMENTS
- Weekly delivery schedule to all 15 hospitals
- Delivery vehicles must be food-grade and temperature controlled where required
- Delivery between 6:00 AM to 10:00 AM only

5. CONTRACT PERIOD: 12 months with option to extend for 6 months.

6. ESTIMATED ANNUAL VALUE: Rs. 4.2 Crores

7. EARNEST MONEY DEPOSIT: Rs. 8.4 Lakhs
