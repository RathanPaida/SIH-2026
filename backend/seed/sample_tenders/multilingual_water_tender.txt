निविदा सूचना — पैकेज्ड पेयजल एवं पीवीसी पाइप आपूर्ति
Tender Ref: WS/2026/0089
Subject: Supply of Packaged Drinking Water and PVC Pipes for Water Supply System

1. कार्य का विवरण (Scope of Work)
जिला मुख्यालय भवन हेतु पैकेज्ड पेयजल की आपूर्ति एवं जल वितरण प्रणाली हेतु पीवीसी पाइप की आपूर्ति एवं स्थापना।
Supply of packaged drinking water for district headquarters building and supply and installation of PVC pipes for water distribution system.

2. तकनीकी विनिर्देश (Technical Specifications)

2.1 पैकेज्ड पेयजल (Packaged Drinking Water)
- पैकेज्ड पेयजल IS 14543 के अनुसार होना चाहिए
- Packaged drinking water shall conform to IS 14543
- 1 लीटर और 20 लीटर बोतलों में आपूर्ति
- Supply in 1 litre and 20 litre bottles
- ISI मार्क अनिवार्य
- ISI mark mandatory
- जल गुणवत्ता IS 10500 के मानकों के अनुरूप हो
- Water quality shall conform to IS 10500 standards
- pH value shall be between 6.5 and 8.5 as per standard test methods

2.2 पीवीसी पाइप (PVC Pipes)
- uPVC pipes for cold water supply shall conform to IS 4985
- CPVC pipes for hot water distribution as per IS 15778
- All pipes shall be ISI marked and of approved make
- Pressure class: minimum 6 kg/sq cm working pressure
- पाइप व्यास: 20mm, 25mm, 32mm, 50mm, 75mm
- Pipe diameters: 20mm, 25mm, 32mm, 50mm, 75mm

2.3 जल वितरण प्रणाली (Water Distribution System)
- Complete water supply and distribution system as per IS 1172
- Storage tank capacity: 50,000 litres overhead tank
- Booster pump set: 5 HP, suitable for building water supply
- All fittings shall be of standard make and ISI marked where applicable

3. सामान्य शर्तें (General Conditions)
- All materials shall be of good quality and conform to latest Indian Standards
- Contractor shall submit quality test reports
- Supply shall be completed within 60 days
- गारंटी अवधि: 12 माह
- Guarantee period: 12 months
