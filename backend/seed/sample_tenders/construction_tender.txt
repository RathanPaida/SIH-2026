TENDER NOTICE

Subject: Construction of Multi-Purpose Government Office Building at Sector 15, Chandigarh

Tender Reference No: PWD/CHD/2025/BUILD-0147

1. SCOPE OF WORK
The Public Works Department, Chandigarh invites sealed tenders for the construction of a G+4 multi-purpose government office building at Sector 15, Chandigarh. The work includes civil construction, electrical installations, plumbing, fire safety systems, and interior finishing.

2. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

2.1 STRUCTURAL REQUIREMENTS
- The building shall be designed for earthquake Zone IV as per relevant Indian Standards for seismic design.
- Reinforced cement concrete (RCC) frame structure using M30 grade concrete for columns and M25 for slabs and beams.
- Ordinary Portland Cement of 43 grade conforming to applicable IS specifications shall be used.
- High strength deformed TMT steel bars (Fe 500D grade) shall be used for reinforcement.
- Concrete mix design shall follow IS guidelines for mix proportioning.
- Coarse and fine aggregates shall conform to relevant IS specifications for concrete aggregates.

2.2 ELECTRICAL INSTALLATIONS
- Complete electrical wiring as per IS code of practice for electrical installations.
- PVC insulated copper cables for internal wiring rated for 1100V working voltage.
- Proper earthing system as per IS standards for earthing of electrical installations.
- LED luminaires for all general lighting requirements conforming to IS specifications.
- UPS system (20 KVA) for critical areas including server room, conforming to IS standards for UPS safety.

2.3 PLUMBING AND WATER SUPPLY
- Water supply system designed as per IS code for water supply and sanitation requirements.
- CPVC pipes for hot water lines and uPVC pipes for cold water distribution.
- All water supplied shall meet IS standards for drinking water quality.

2.4 FIRE SAFETY
- Fire detection and alarm system as per IS code of practice for fire detection systems.
- Portable fire extinguishers (ABC type, CO2 type) conforming to IS specifications at all floors.
- Building design shall comply with IS code of practice for fire safety of buildings.
- Fire escape routes and emergency exits as per building codes.

2.5 SAFETY REQUIREMENTS
- All construction workers must be provided with safety helmets conforming to IS specifications for industrial safety helmets.
- Safety shoes and eye protectors as per relevant IS standards.

3. QUALITY ASSURANCE
- Concrete strength shall be verified by cube testing as per IS test methods for concrete strength.
- All materials shall be ISI marked where applicable.
- Third party quality audit shall be conducted at every major milestone.

4. COMPLETION PERIOD: 18 months from date of award.

5. ESTIMATED COST: Rs. 12.5 Crores

6. EARNEST MONEY DEPOSIT: Rs. 25 Lakhs
