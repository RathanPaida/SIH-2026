TENDER DOCUMENT

Subject: Procurement of IT Equipment and Networking Infrastructure for District Collectorate, Pune

Tender Reference No: NIC/MH/2025/IT-0389

1. SCOPE OF PROCUREMENT
The National Informatics Centre (NIC), Maharashtra State Unit invites tenders for supply, installation, and commissioning of IT equipment and networking infrastructure at the District Collectorate, Pune.

2. ITEM SPECIFICATIONS

2.1 DESKTOP COMPUTERS (Quantity: 150)
- Processor: Intel Core i5 (12th Gen or higher) or equivalent
- RAM: 16 GB DDR4
- Storage: 512 GB SSD
- Display: 23.8 inch Full HD LED/LCD monitor conforming to IS specifications for display monitors
- Operating System: Pre-loaded with licensed Windows 11 Professional
- All IT equipment shall meet safety requirements as per IS standards for information technology equipment safety
- Keyboard and mouse (USB wired)

2.2 NETWORK INFRASTRUCTURE
- Managed Layer 3 Network Switches (48-port Gigabit): 10 nos. conforming to IS specifications for network switches
- Managed Layer 2 Access Switches (24-port): 20 nos.
- Enterprise Routers with firewall capability: 2 nos.
- Structured cabling (Cat 6A) as per IS standards for data centre cabling
- Network racks (42U): 3 nos. with proper cable management

2.3 UPS AND POWER BACKUP
- Online UPS System (60 KVA) with 30 minutes battery backup conforming to IS specifications for UPS safety and general requirements
- UPS shall comply with EMC requirements as per applicable IS standards
- Proper earthing for all IT equipment as per IS standards for earthing

2.4 SERVER ROOM
- Precision air conditioning (2 ton): 2 nos.
- Raised flooring as per data centre standards
- Fire suppression system (gas-based) with fire detection conforming to IS codes

3. INSTALLATION REQUIREMENTS
- Complete structured cabling with proper labelling and documentation
- All electrical wiring for IT equipment as per IS code of practice for electrical wiring
- PVC insulated cables for power distribution as per relevant IS specifications

4. WARRANTY: 5 years comprehensive on-site warranty for all equipment.

5. COMPLETION PERIOD: 90 days from date of purchase order.

6. ESTIMATED COST: Rs. 2.8 Crores
