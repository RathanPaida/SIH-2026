TENDER NOTICE — ELECTRICAL WORKS
Tender Ref: EW/2026/0047
Subject: Supply and Installation of Electrical Cables, Switches and Socket-Outlets

1. SCOPE OF WORK
Supply and installation of electrical wiring system for a new government office building, Block-C.

2. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

2.1 PVC Insulated Cables
All PVC insulated cables shall conform to IS 694 and shall be suitable for working voltages up to 1100V. Cables shall be of copper conductor, PVC insulated with following ratings:
- 1.5 sq mm for lighting circuits
- 2.5 sq mm for power socket circuits  
- 4.0 sq mm for high power appliances
- 6.0 sq mm for sub-main cables
All cables shall be ISI marked.

2.2 XLPE Cables
XLPE insulated PVC sheathed cables as per IS 7098 shall be used for main feeders. Cables shall be armoured type, suitable for direct burial and outdoor installation. Cross-section shall be as per approved electrical drawings.

2.3 Switches
Modular type piano switches conforming to relevant Indian Standards shall be supplied. Switches shall be rated 6A and 16A, 250V AC. All switches shall be from approved manufacturers.

2.4 Socket-Outlets
5-pin and 6-pin socket-outlets shall be provided as per electrical layout. Socket-outlets shall be flush mounted type, rated 6A/16A, 250V.

2.5 Earthing System
Complete earthing system shall be provided as per IS 3043:2018. Minimum two earth pits with GI pipe electrodes shall be installed. Earth resistance shall not exceed 1 ohm.

2.6 Wiring Installation
All electrical wiring installation shall conform to IS 732:2019. Concealed PVC conduit wiring system shall be adopted. All joints shall be made in junction boxes.

2.7 Distribution Boards and MCBs
Distribution boards shall be of sheet steel, powder coated, wall mounted type. MCBs shall be of breaking capacity 10kA minimum.

2.8 Power Transformer
One 250 KVA oil-filled distribution transformer shall be supplied and installed at the substation. Installation shall be as per relevant codes.

3. GENERAL CONDITIONS
- All materials shall be of best quality and shall conform to latest Indian Standards
- Contractor shall submit test certificates for all materials
- Work shall be completed within 120 days from date of order
- Guarantee period: 12 months from date of completion
